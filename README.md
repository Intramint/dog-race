Dog racing simulator with betting. Each dog rolls random stats once, but the outcome can vary due to a random chance of speeding up or slowing down during the race. Dogs have relatively similar chances of winning, but the faster ones are more likely to. Different stats influence acceleration, top speed and stamina, which makes races fun to watch.
